IMPORTANT
- An Alternate Art or Overnumbered printing remains eligible even when its
  underlying rarity metadata says Rare. It is included because it is AA/ON.
- Plain standard Rare printings are not included anywhere on Board 10.
- Copy Listing identifies every spot by exact card name, collector number, and
  treatment.
- Regular Epics and Astral Heron use the Heart popup; any standalone Rare uses
  the Construction popup. These visual rules apply to Board 10 only.
- The Static OBS Board uses five dense columns. A sold position makes a short
  downward crash, then the following cards advance through a small staggered
  cascade to fill the open grid cell. The whole 106-card board is never
  animated at once.
- Signature claims use the premium sapphire/platinum crowned-diamond portal.
- Six dedicated Board 10 statue images have SIG, OVERNUMBERED, ALTERNATE ART,
  EPIC, SP, or RARE physically baked into their built-in plates. The separate
  top buyer copy and external bottom title are
  visually transparent on Board 10, but their information remains intact in
  the popup markup. The complete name and collector number remain in Copy
  Listing, and no ledger or assignment data is changed.
- The visible rarity words are part of the statue artwork itself, centered in
  the large wide nameplate, and scale together when Statue Size changes.
- Installing this preset replaces saved Board 10 only. Board 9, other presets,
  the live ledger, pending rounds, Buyer Bags, history, and connector data are
  not modified.
