BreakSuite6 VIP Chat Alert Connector v0.3.37

This connector reads the current Whatnot chat while always-on sync is connected.
It sends a private alert to BreakSuite6 when a person appearing in chat has at
least $10 in saved Orders History purchases. It never posts or sends a message
to Whatnot.

Install this connector folder from chrome://extensions or brave://extensions:
1. Turn on Developer mode.
2. Click Load unpacked.
3. Choose this BreakSuite6_Whatnot_Connector folder.
4. Open your one correct Whatnot Live Manager tab. It locks automatically.
