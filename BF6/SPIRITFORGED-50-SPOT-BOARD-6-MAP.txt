OWNERSHIP RULES
- Signature lanes own the exact Signature printing shown in the title.
- For Teemo, Sett, Darius, Vayne, Aphelios, Yone, Soraka, Ahri, Bard, Yasuo, and Karma, the matching ON lane owns the Overnumbered plus the remaining champion-family printings; the Signature is excluded.
- Irelia, Fervent is a three-way split: Signature only, Overnumbered only, and ALT SFD-057A/221 + EPIC SFD-057/221 only.
- Irelia, Blade Dancer owns the remaining non-Fervent Irelia family.
- Rumble, Lucian, Draven, Rek'Sai, Ornn, Jax, Azir, Ezreal, Renata Glasc, Sivir, and Fiora keep their matching champion family in their ON lane.
- Each Seal remains its own standalone chase spot.
- Spot 44 owns all Spiritforged Showcase / Alternate-Art Runes. Regular Common Runes are not mapped hits.
- Spots 45-50 contain Rare/Epic non-champion Gear, Spell, and Unit cards only, grouped by matching domain/color.
- Premonition goes to Mind. Last Rites, Downwell, and Switcheroo go to Chaos. Other qualifying cards follow their matching domain/color.
- Champion Units such as Fizz are excluded from the six Rare+ non-champion color pools.
- Dual-domain Rare/Epic cards follow their first printed domain so one card can never belong to two buyers.
- Common/Uncommon cards are not part of the mapped hit spots.
