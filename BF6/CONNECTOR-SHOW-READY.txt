CONNECTOR SHOW READY UPDATE
1. Extract this ZIP and run RUN-THIS-UPDATE.cmd with BreakSuite closed.
2. In Chrome/Brave Extensions, reload the existing BreakSuite6 Whatnot Connector. If its folder is elsewhere, load the updated app/BreakSuite6_Whatnot_Connector folder instead, and disable the older copy.
3. Refresh your Whatnot Live Manager tab once after updating the extension. Keep BreakSuite and OBS open, save the correct board, then click Prepare Show before the first sale.
4. Wait for SHOW READY. The connector locks the selected show tab and verifies the current-round starting count. Start numbered spot listings; new sales are watched automatically.
5. For missed spots or exchanges, click Recover / Recheck List once and wait. It internally retries incomplete scans, updates Buyer Bags and the OBS board, and reports recovered/exchanged/returned counts.
If Assigned is incomplete or changing, no reconciliation is applied. Advanced tools remain available. Recovery never resets the round boundary. If the original boundary was lost before startup, the existing count-based recovery cannot infer which historical rows belong to the box; use the explicit newest-row recovery under Advanced only when you know that count.
Includes the previous overlay scrollbar and smooth-slide fixes.
